Usage: ab-av1 <COMMAND>

Commands:
  auto-encode    Automatically find the best CRF for --min-vmaf and encode
  crf-search     Find the best CRF delivering --min-vmaf
  sample-encode  Encode short samples to predict VMAF / size / time
  encode         Encode with a given CRF & preset
  vmaf           Full VMAF score (reference vs distorted)
  xpsnr          Full XPSNR score (reference vs distorted)
  help           Print this message or the help of the given subcommand(s)

Options:
  -h, --help     Print help
  -V, --version  Print version
